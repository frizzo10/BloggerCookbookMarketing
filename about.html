About Blogger Cookbook

Get paid. Get reach. Fight scrapers.

Blogger Cookbook connects food bloggers' recipes to families using Fern, a personal AI meal-planning app. When a family follows your blog and cooks your recipes through Fern, you're credited by name on every touchpoint, linked back to your original post, and paid 30% of the subscription revenue your content drives.

Why we built this

Scraper sites copy food bloggers' recipes daily, republish them without credit, and often outrank the original creator on Google — all while paying nothing. Blogger Cookbook is built as the opposite: full attribution, a direct link back to your site on every interaction, and a real revenue share, not a one-time fee.

How it works

Bloggers apply for a free listing. Once approved, your recipes become discoverable to Fern's users, matched to families likely to enjoy your specific style of cooking. Every recipe carries your name and a link to your original post. When your content drives a family to subscribe to Fern Pro or Pro Max, you earn 30% of that subscription, recurring, for as long as they stay subscribed.

Brand Studio

Approved bloggers can also embed a Brand Studio widget directly on their own site — a branded, interactive recipe card that links back to Fern and helps readers add your recipes to their own meal plan in one tap.
