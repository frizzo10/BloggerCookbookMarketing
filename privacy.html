Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Blogger Cookbook ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Blogger Cookbook website, signup application, and Brand Studio widget (together, the "Service").

Information We Collect

Account information: When you apply to list your blog, we collect your name, email address, blog URL, and content specialty.

Recipe and content data: Recipe URLs and content you submit for listing, which we use to surface your recipes to families using Fern.

Attribution and revenue data: We track when your content is viewed, saved, or drives a subscription within Fern, in order to calculate and pay your revenue share accurately.

Payment information: If you're approved and earn revenue share, we collect the payment details necessary to pay you (such as a payout account), processed through our payment provider.

Brand Studio widget data: If you embed the Brand Studio widget on your own site, we collect engagement data (views, saves, clicks) from visitors interacting with the widget on your site.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to review and approve blog listings, surface your content to relevant families in Fern, calculate and pay revenue share, operate the Brand Studio widget, and communicate with you about your account.

Third-Party Services

Blogger Cookbook relies on the following third-party services:

  • Fern (myaifern.com), the consumer app your recipes are surfaced within
  • Supabase, for account authentication and data storage
  • A payment processor, for revenue share payouts
  • Netlify, for hosting the Service

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and content data for as long as your listing is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below, subject to any records we're required to retain for payment or tax purposes.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to hello@bloggercookbook.com.
